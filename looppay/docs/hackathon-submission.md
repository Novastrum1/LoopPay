LoopPay turns a stablecoin merchant payment into a fully funded, interoperable loyalty credit. Checkout settles directly to the merchant. Customers redeem non-transferable credits with opted-in Loop Network partners; the issuing campaign reserve settles to the receiving merchant. Merchants get QR invoices, campaign controls, and indexed returning-wallet analytics without a paid indexer.
