# Small merchant pilot

Recruit a small, explicitly consented cohort (target 3–5 independent merchants; recruitment has not been claimed). Explain test tokens, irreversible payments, current payout checks, funded reserves, permanent closure, protected earned liabilities and practical support. Use public business metadata only. Record consent privately with minimal retention; no customer identity profiling.

Start on testnet with separate test wallets and faucet gas. Each merchant practices registration, safe payout review, exact funding, QR invoice, a customer purchase, partner opt-in, redemption, close and refund. Collect consented feedback on clarity, time, failures and reserve economics. Finish real-device wallet QA and independent security review before any separately approved mainnet pilot. No real funds are needed for development.

Define activation as profile + funded campaign + first eligible settled invoice. For each reporting period record actual deployment, token identity, block range, payment count and exact-unit volume, active/returning public wallet addresses, reward earning, redemption and cross-merchant redemption, confirmation time and merchant feedback. A wallet is not a unique person. Separate gross, eligible, reward-earning and excluded transactions; report test/self/team/mock/synthetic activity separately. Testnet rehearsals never count as production traction.

Days 1–30: recruit and obtain consent, rehearse testnet, measure onboarding friction and failure reasons. Days 31–60: assess return-address cohorts, partner redemption and reserve sustainability on the approved environment. Days 61–90: review retention and economics with merchants; decide whether to expand only from observed evidence. These are planned windows, not achieved milestones or commitments.

Publish only aggregates with cohort definitions, denominators and exclusions. Leave numbers pending until measured. Provide an exit route: stop earning, close campaign, refund unused funds, preserve already-earned redemption rights and explain partner availability. Merchants must understand that admin pause can temporarily interrupt use and refunds do not reclaim earned credits.
